# WardZero — Product context (inferred from the Operation Ward Zero brief)

Audience: hackathon judges (Wooble) evaluating end-to-end integration, technical skill,
reliability, practical implementation, and explainability/clinical safety; secondarily,
family caregivers as the product's end users.

Job of the surface (Operate mode): let a caregiver grasp a home patient's state in
seconds — are they stable, what is driving the risk, who has responded — and let a judge
verify the full Sense → Understand → Predict → Alert → Respond loop in one sitting.

Tone: calm, precise, honest. Synthetic data is labelled everywhere; the risk engine is
described as rule-based, never as medical AI. Assumptions (subagent build, brief-supplied,
no interview possible): one patient, browser-only state, accelerated simulation time.
